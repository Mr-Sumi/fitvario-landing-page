## What to look for

Score each tool from 0 to 3 on these, and weight them for your gym.

| Criterion | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Membership and renewals | Plans, renewals, freezes, joining and expiry dates |
| Billing | Invoices, part payments, tax lines, due lists |
| Attendance | QR, manual, biometric, and how check-in is recorded |
| Leads and follow-up | Enquiry list, follow-up dates, a daily list |
| Messaging | WhatsApp, SMS and push, and what each message costs |
| Reports | Collections, expenses, member growth, branch comparison |
| Staff access | What each person can see and do |
| Member app | Whether members get an app, and whose name is on it |
| Data | Can you export everything? Who owns it? |
| Support | Hours, language, how fast |
| Price | Per member, per branch or flat, plus extras |

## How to run a fair trial

1. List your ten most common daily tasks.
2. Ask each vendor to show those ten, not their own demo.
3. Try them with a few real members and a real week.
4. Ask your front-desk person which was easiest. They will use it more than you.
5. Check the export before you commit.

## Points that matter in India

- **GST and invoices:** can it add the tax lines you need, and issue an invoice you can hand over?
- **UPI and Razorpay:** how do you record a UPI payment, and can members pay online?
- **WhatsApp:** most members live there, so ask how messages are sent and charged.
- **Hindi and Hinglish:** whether the app and messages work in the language your members use.
- **Low-end phones:** members may use older Android phones, so the app must be light.

## A scoring worksheet

For each tool, write a score from 0 to 3 against the criteria above, multiply by your weight (1 to 3) and add up. The total is a conversation aid, not a verdict. If two tools are close, the deciding factor should be support and how the trial felt.

## Questions owners ask

### Should I pick the tool with the most features?

No. Pick the one that does your ten daily jobs well. A long feature list is mostly noise if your team will only use a few.

### How long should a trial last?

Long enough to run a full renewal cycle on a few real members, which is often a month. A 15-minute demo shows the best path; a real week shows the daily friction.

### What matters more, price or support?

For a small team, support. When something breaks on a busy evening, a fast answer in your language is worth more than a small saving.

### What if I switch later?

Check the export now. A tool that lets you leave easily is usually one you can trust.
