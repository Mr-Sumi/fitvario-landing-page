> This is a template for general guidance and not legal advice. Have it reviewed by your own advisor before you use it, and adjust it to your gym, your state and your plans.

A freeze keeps a member on your books during a break. Members who travel, recover from an injury or have an exam period often quit when there is no pause option. A clear policy lets you say yes without being unfair to anyone.

## Decisions to make first

| Question | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Who can freeze? | Everyone, or only plans of 3 months and above |
| How long? | A minimum and a maximum, for example 7 days to 30 days at a time |
| How often? | Once per plan, or a total number of days per plan |
| Does it cost? | Free, or a small admin fee |
| Proof needed? | None, or a note for medical reasons |
| When does it start? | From the day requested, never backdated |

## A policy you can adapt

**1. Eligibility.** Members on plans of {minimum plan length} or longer may request a freeze.

**2. Length.** A freeze must be for at least {minimum days} days and no more than {maximum days} days in one request. The total frozen time in any plan may not exceed {total days} days.

**3. Fee.** There is {no fee / a fee of ₹{amount}} for each freeze.

**4. How to request.** The member asks at the desk or messages the gym before the freeze starts. The gym confirms the start date and the end date in writing.

**5. Effect.** While a plan is frozen the member cannot use the facilities. When the freeze ends, the plan is extended by the number of days frozen.

**6. No backdating.** A freeze starts on the day it is confirmed. Days already missed cannot be frozen afterwards.

**7. Medical freezes.** For a medical reason, the gym may ask for a doctor's note and may allow a longer freeze at its discretion.

**8. Ending early.** A member may end a freeze early by telling the gym. The plan resumes from that day.

**9. Changes.** The gym may change this policy by informing members in advance. A change does not affect a freeze that has already been confirmed.

## Record every freeze

For each freeze, write down the member, the start date, the end date, the new plan end date and who approved it. A freeze that is not recorded is the source of most later disputes.

## Pause or discount?

A member who says "it is too expensive right now" may need a smaller plan rather than a freeze. Ask what is behind the request before offering either. A short conversation often shows whether the member intends to return.

## Tell members the option exists

Mention the policy in your joining paperwork and on the notice board, and say it out loud when a member mentions travel or injury. A freeze offered early keeps people on your books. One discovered after they quit helps nobody.

## Sample notices

For the notice board:

> **Freezing your membership.** Going away or recovering from an injury? Ask at the desk for a freeze of up to {maximum days} days. Your plan will be extended by the days frozen. Please ask before the freeze begins.

For a confirmation message:

> Hi {name}, your freeze is confirmed from {start date} to {end date}. Your plan will now end on {new end date}. See you when you are back!

## Edge cases to decide in advance

- **A freeze that falls inside a free-days offer.** Decide whether bonus days can be frozen. Most gyms say they follow the plan's normal rules.
- **A member who freezes right before expiry.** Allow it only if the freeze starts before the plan ends, and extend the plan by the frozen days.
- **A family or couple plan.** Decide whether both people must freeze together or can freeze separately.
- **Personal training packages.** Usually the sessions are valid for a fixed period. State whether a freeze extends that period too.
- **A freeze that is never ended.** Set an automatic end date so a pause does not become an open-ended hold.

## Questions owners ask

### Should I charge for a freeze?

You can, if administration time is real or if you want to discourage short, frequent freezes. A small fee for the second freeze in a plan is common. Free freezes for medical reasons build goodwill.

### Can a member freeze twice?

Yes, if your total cap allows it. The total number of days in a plan is the number that matters more than the number of requests.

### Does a freeze count as a refund?

No. A freeze extends the plan; a refund returns money. Keep the two policies separate and say so in writing.

### Where should the policy live?

On the joining form, on a notice at the desk and in any message you send when someone mentions a break. A policy that is hard to find is not much of a policy.
