Most members who let a plan lapse did not decide to leave. They were busy, they forgot the date, or nobody asked. A short, friendly message at the right moment turns many of those into renewals. This post gives you the timing, eight templates and the rules that keep the message from sounding like a debt notice.

These are templates you send yourself, from the gym's phone or from whichever tool you use. Change the words so they sound like you.

## When to send a renewal reminder

Think of renewal as a short sequence rather than one message.

| When | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| 7 days before expiry | Early heads-up, time to decide |
| 3 days before | Gentle nudge, mention the offer if any |
| 1 day before, or the last day | Clear and direct: this is the last day |
| 1 to 2 days after expiry | Door still open, say how to restart |
| About 2 weeks after | Win-back, ask what would help |

Do not send all five to everyone. If a member renews after the first message, stop. The sequence only continues for people who have not replied.

## Eight templates

Replace the words in curly brackets with the member's details.

### 1. Early heads-up (7 days before)

> Hi {name}, this is {gym name}. Your membership runs until {date}. Would you like to renew for the same plan, or look at a longer one? Reply here and we will sort it at the desk. See you at the gym!

### 2. Three days before

> Hi {name}, a quick reminder that your plan ends on {date}. If you renew before then, {offer or "your slot and trainer stay the same"}. You can pay at the desk or by UPI on {UPI number}.

### 3. Last day

> Hi {name}, your membership at {gym name} ends today. To keep training without a break, renew at the desk or pay by UPI today. Reply if you need any help.

### 4. The day after expiry

> Hi {name}, your plan ended yesterday. No problem, you can restart whenever you are ready. Renew in the next 3 days and we will count it from the same date, so you do not lose any days.

### 5. Two weeks later (win-back)

> Hi {name}, we have not seen you for a couple of weeks and we miss you at {gym name}. Is anything getting in the way, like timings or a change in routine? Tell us and we will try to fix it.

### 6. With an offer

> Hi {name}, this month we have {offer} for members who renew a 3-month plan before {date}. It is valid only till then. Reply YES and we will hold it for you.

### 7. For someone who has been on a freeze

> Hi {name}, your freeze ends on {date}. Your plan will start again from that day. If you want to change your timings or plan, tell us before then and we will arrange it.

### 8. Thank you after renewing

> Thank you {name}, you are all set until {new date}. Your next plan ends on {new date}. See you tomorrow!

## What makes a renewal message work

- **Use the name and the date.** A message with both reads as personal and is hard to ignore.
- **Say the next step.** "Reply here", "pay by UPI" or "come to the desk" removes the effort of working out what to do.
- **Keep it short.** If it does not fit on the screen without scrolling, it is too long.
- **Offer something real.** If you mention an offer, state it and its end date.
- **Send from a number members recognise.** A message from a saved gym number is easier to trust than one from an unknown number.

## What to avoid

- Do not threaten or hint at penalties in a renewal message. Keep money talk for the dues conversation.
- Do not send the same text to a whole list at once. Replace the name and date, or the message looks automated.
- Do not message late at night or before early morning.
- Do not keep messaging someone who replied "not now". Note it and try again in a month.

## Keeping track

Keep one list with three columns: member, expiry date and what you sent. A shared sheet works for a small gym. A CRM list does the same job and shows who is due today, so no one depends on memory. ## A simple routine for the desk

1. Every morning, list members whose plan ends in 7, 3 or 1 days, or ended yesterday.
2. Send the matching template.
3. Note the reply, or mark "no reply".
4. At the end of the week, call the ones who still have not replied.

## Questions owners ask

### How many reminders is too many?

Three before expiry and one or two after is plenty. A fourth message before expiry rarely helps and starts to irritate. If someone has not replied after the last-day message, a short call is more respectful than another text.

### Should I send a message or call?

Use a message for the first two touches, because it is easy to answer when the person is free. Use a call for the last day and for members you know well. A call also lets you hear the reason, and the reason is often the most useful thing you learn.

### What if the member replies "I will pay later"?

Thank them and ask for a date: "No problem, shall I note Thursday?" Write that date next to their name. If it passes, you have a natural reason to message again, and the member knows you remembered.

### Should I offer a discount to renew?

Only when it moves the member to a longer plan. A discount for renewing the same short plan teaches everyone to wait for it. A small saving on three months, offered before expiry, rewards commitment instead.

### What about members who never reply?

Keep a note and try one phone call. Silence often means busy, not unhappy. If the call goes unanswered, leave the door open with the win-back message two weeks later and then stop. A short, kind last message is enough.
