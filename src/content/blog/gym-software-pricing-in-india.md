Software prices are easy to misread. A low monthly number can hide a charge per member, a setup fee or a messaging bill. This guide shows the models you will meet and what to ask.

## The common pricing models

| Model | How it works | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Per member | A fee for each active member | Cost rises as you grow, so check how "active" is defined |
| Per branch | A fee for each location | Fine for a single gym, add up for a chain |
| Flat fee | One price regardless of size | Often capped, so ask what the cap is |
| Tiers | Plans with limits on members, staff or features | The limit you hit first |
| Usage | Credits for messages or storage | The real monthly bill can be higher than the base fee |

## What drives the price

- **Branches:** more locations mean more setup, more staff access and more reporting.
- **Members:** the number you manage at one time.
- **Messaging:** WhatsApp, SMS and push usually run on credits. A gym that reminds every member sends many more messages than one that sends a few.
- **Modules:** attendance, CRM, billing, member apps and reports may be priced separately.
- **Support and setup:** onboarding, data import and training can be included or charged.

## Costs people forget

1. Setup or data import fees.
2. Messaging credits on top of the plan.
3. GST on the quote.
4. Charges for extra staff accounts or branches.
5. The cost of leaving: can you export your member and payment data?

## Questions to ask every vendor

- Is the price per member, per branch or a flat fee, and what happens when you grow by 100 members?
- Are messaging credits included, and how many does your size need?
- Is there a setup fee, and what does it cover?
- Can you export all your data at any time, in a standard format?
- What is the refund and cancellation policy?
- Who answers when something breaks, and in which languages and hours?

## Work out the cost for a year

Multiply the monthly price by twelve, add setup, add messaging for your expected volume and add GST. Divide by your member count to get a cost per member per month. Compare vendors on that number, not on the headline. Then ask whether the software saves you more than that: fewer lapsed renewals, quicker collections and less time spent on registers.

## A worked comparison

Suppose a gym with 250 members compares two quotes. Quote A is ₹3,000 a month plus ₹2,000 for messaging, with a ₹10,000 setup fee. Quote B is ₹5,500 a month with messaging included and no setup fee.

For the first year, Quote A is ₹3,000 × 12 = ₹36,000, plus ₹24,000 for messaging, plus ₹10,000 setup, which is ₹70,000. Quote B is ₹5,500 × 12 = ₹66,000. The cheaper-looking monthly number costs more over the year, and the gap changes again if you send more or fewer messages. These figures are invented to show the method. Use real quotes.

## Questions owners ask

### Is free software worth it?

A free tool can be enough for a very small gym, as long as you know the limits: members, messaging, support, and whether you can export your data. Check what happens when you outgrow it.

### Should I pay annually?

An annual payment often lowers the monthly cost, but you commit before you know whether the software fits. If you can, run a month or a quarter first, then move to annual with a clear refund policy in writing.

### What does "unlimited" really mean?

Ask for the definition. "Unlimited members" may still cap staff accounts, branches or messages. Get the limits in writing.

### Who owns my member data?

You should. Confirm that you can export all of it at any time, and what happens to it if you leave.

### How do I compare a global product with an Indian one?

Look at rupee pricing, GST invoices, UPI and WhatsApp support, support hours and language, and local references. A product priced in dollars may swing with exchange rates.

## Contract terms worth reading

Read three clauses before you sign: the minimum term and how to cancel, what happens to your data when the plan ends, and whether the price can change mid-term. A low price with a long lock-in and no export is a cost you pay later.
